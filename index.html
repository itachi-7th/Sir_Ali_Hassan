Chemistry / Computer

You taught us that everything has a system — from atoms to algorithms.

Move across the field, or touch and drag. Tap to start a reaction.

Matter

It starts with parts.

Atoms bond into molecules. Molecules build everything we can touch. Here, the field is drawn as an artistic model, not a simulation. Pick an element to find it.

Machine

Then the parts become logic.

Nodes, links, signals. The same parts, now arranged on a grid, passing information along every connection. Tap anywhere to send a pulse through the system.

The fusion

One idea, two languages.

Both subjects ask how individual parts interact to make something larger. Drag the slider and watch one structure change its language.
